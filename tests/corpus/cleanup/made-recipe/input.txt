Lemon and Thyme Roast Chicken
Serves four. Preparation 20 minutes, cooking 1 hour 30 minutes.
A simple roast that rewards a little patience. The lemon keeps the meat moist while the thyme perfumes the
skin, and the vegetables cook in the juices underneath, so the whole meal comes out of one tin. Leave the
chicken to rest before carving; it makes all the difference to how tender it is.
Ingredients
• 1 whole chicken, about 1.6 kg
• 2 unwaxed lemons
• A small bunch of fresh thyme
• 4 cloves of garlic, unpeeled
• 800 g floury potatoes, cut into chunks
• 3 carrots, halved lengthways
• 2 tablespoons of olive oil
• Salt and black pepper
Method
1. Heat the oven to 200 degrees, or 180 for a fan oven. Take the chicken out of the fridge half an hour be-
fore you start, so that it cooks evenly.
2. Halve one lemon and push it into the cavity with half the thyme and the garlic. Slice the other lemon
and lay the slices in the bottom of a large roasting tin.
3. Toss the potatoes and carrots in the oil with a good pinch of salt, and spread them around the lemon
slices. Sit the chicken on top and season the skin generously.
4. Roast for an hour and a half, basting twice, until the juices run clear when the thigh is pierced. Rest the
chicken under foil for fifteen minutes while the vegetables finish crisping in the oven.
