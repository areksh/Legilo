Terms and Conditions of Membership
1. Definitions
1.1 In these terms, the Club means the Riverside Rowing Club, a company limited by guarantee, and the
Member means any person whose application for membership has been accepted by the committee and
whose subscription is fully paid.
1.2 The Premises means the boathouse, the landing stage and any other land or buildings that the Club owns,
leases or is permitted to use from time to time.
2. Subscriptions
2.1 Annual subscriptions fall due on the first day of April each year. A Member who has not paid within sixty
days of that date shall be treated as having resigned, unless the committee agrees otherwise in writing.
2.2 The committee may vary the rates of subscription at the annual general meeting, and shall give Members
at least twenty-eight days' notice of any proposed change.
3. Use of Equipment
3.1 Members may use the Club's boats and equipment only after completing the safety course and only dur-
ing the hours set out in the boathouse rules, as amended from time to time.
3.2 A Member who damages any boat or equipment through negligence shall be liable for the reasonable cost
of its repair or replacement, as determined by the committee.
