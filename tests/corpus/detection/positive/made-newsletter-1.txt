BOOK I. (Folio) CHAPTER IV.
(Hump Back).—This whale is often
seen on the northern American coast.
He has been frequently captured there,
and towed into harbor. He has a great
pack on him like a peddler; or you
might call him the Elephant and Castle
whale. At any rate, the popular name
for him does not sufficiently distinguish him, since the sperm whale also
has a hump though a smaller one. His
oil is not very valuable. He has baleen.
