3.5 ENCODING MODELS
We first fit ridge encoding models using the full embedding space to establish a reference perfor-
mance for each model and layer. For iEEG, word embeddings predicted electrode responses at sam-
pled lags relative to word onset, with performance evaluated using five contiguous held-out word
folds. For fMRI, embeddings were aligned to acquisition times and used to construct a four-delay
finite impulse response design; models were evaluated on held-out stories. Ridge penalties were
selected by cross-validation.
3.6 REDUCED-RANK REGRESSION
We used ridge reduced-rank regression (RRR) (Izenman, 1975) to identify the optimal rank k sub-
space of embeddings for neural prediction (Figure 1b). Within each training partition, we fit a
multivariate ridge model from embeddings (X) to neural responses (Y ), producing coefficients (W)
and fitted responses (Ŷ = XW). We decomposed (Ŷ ) and used its leading (k) right singular vec-
tors (Vk) to construct an input factor (Ak = WVk), whose columns span embedding directions
associated with the leading components of predicted neural activity.
