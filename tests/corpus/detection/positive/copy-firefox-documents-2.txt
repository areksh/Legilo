Brown Treesnakes

The brown treesnake (BTS)

was accidentally introduced to

Guam in the late 1940s and has

caused global or island-wide

extinction of 9 of 13 native forest

birds (Fritts, 1988). This invader

frequently short circuits power

lines, causing costly power

outages and is a significant risk for

further introductions regionally

and nationally. The USGS has

played an essential role in devel
-

oping control techniques such as

traps and barriers to manage this

pest and leads the regional BTS

Rapid Response Team to ensure

effective early detection and rapid

response to BTS sightings in new

locations on the Pacific Islands.
