3.1.
 
Participants and context

This quasi-experimental study involved 108 second-year non-English major undergraduates (aged between 18–22) in a

south China university, with 54 in the experimental class (EC) and 54 in the control class (CC) under the instruction of the

same teacher. To contextualize the scale and significance of English language learning in China, it is noteworthy that the

country had already surpassed 390 million English learners and users by 2000 [
